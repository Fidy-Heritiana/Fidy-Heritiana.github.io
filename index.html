03 / APPROACH

Between theory and experiment.

I study how the structure and distribution of data influence the behaviour of algorithms. My work draws on generating functions, combinatorial models and probabilistic analysis.

Part of this research concerns oriented hierarchies in statistical implicative analysis. Code and demonstrations make it possible to inspect the constructions and compare models with experiments.
